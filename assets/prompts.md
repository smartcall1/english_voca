파일: `comic-01.png`

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Create one beautiful children's storybook comic page, exactly FOUR equal panels in a 2 by 2 grid, square 1024x1024 image, straight very thin cream gutters precisely at horizontal and vertical centre. Each panel is its own complete scene. Warm professional gouache illustration with clean expressive faces and rich real settings, soft sunlight, appealing for children aged 5-8. Same characters throughout: Suho, young Korean boy with short dark hair in blue T-shirt and navy shorts; Seoha, young Korean girl with dark bob hair in yellow T-shirt and teal shorts; their tiny friendly koala explorer wearing a green neckerchief. Australian primary school classroom. Read panel sequence top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right. Panel 1: At the classroom doorway Seoha smiles and waves hello to nervous newcomer Suho with his blue schoolbag; koala peeks from doorway. Panel 2: Suho's schoolbag has tipped over on the classroom floor; pencils and a red book have fallen out; Suho looks upset and Seoha notices. Panel 3: Seoha kneels and gives Suho the red book as they pick up pencils together; koala holds one pencil. Panel 4: Suho and Seoha happily sit together at a small classroom table drawing one picture with coloured pencils; koala smiles between them, friendship resolved. No words, no lettering, no panel numbers, no speech balloons, no emojis, no logos, no watermark. Full body gestures and setting make the story self explanatory. Keep crisp clear images in all four panels. No outer border.
```
