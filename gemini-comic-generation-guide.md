# Gemini용 50일 독립 4컷 만화 생성 가이드

## 사용 방법

아래 공통 프롬프트를 먼저 붙이고, 이어서 원하는 Day의 개별 프롬프트 한 개를 붙여 Gemini에 입력하시오. Day마다 완전히 별도의 사건으로 생성한다. 다른 Day의 이미지를 참고 이미지로 넣지 말고, 이전 결과를 복사·재배치·색상 변경하지 않는다.

## 공통 프롬프트

Create ONE completely independent children's English learning comic page for the specified DAY. Output exactly one square 1024 x 1024 PNG with four equal square panels in a precise 2x2 grid and a thin cream gutter. Use a warm, clear storybook illustration style with strong visual actions and concrete objects. Characters do not need continuity between days; design the most suitable people, animals, or objects for this day's vocabulary. Every panel must visibly explain its assigned words and sentence meaning through an unambiguous action. Use reading order top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.

The application supplies dialogue separately. Absolutely do not render any text, letters, numbers, labels, signs, logos, speech bubbles, captions, book writing, watermarks, or UI in the image. Do not put readable writing on objects. Do not reuse a previous day's scene, composition, characters, props, or panel arrangement. Do not make generic smiling portraits. Keep all four panels visually distinct while forming one small self-contained event. The four panels should each cover three of the day's twelve vocabulary items, with the listed example sentences guiding the action. Preserve safe, child-friendly behaviour and clear cause-and-effect.

1. celebrate — 축하하다. Example meaning: “We celebrate what we have learnt.”
2. finish — 끝내다. Example meaning: “I can finish this puzzle.”
3. success — 성공. Example meaning: “Finishing the story is a success.”
4. effort — 노력. Example meaning: “Your effort helps you learn.”
5. improve — 나아지다; 향상시키다. Example meaning: “I improve with practice.”
6. patient — 참을성 있는. Example meaning: “Be patient while your friend thinks.”
7. fair — 공평한. Example meaning: “Everyone gets a fair turn.”
8. honest — 정직한. Example meaning: “Be honest about what happened.”
9. promise — 약속하다. Example meaning: “I promise to take care of the book.”
10. invite — 초대하다. Example meaning: “We invite our friends to the picnic.”
11. thank — 감사하다. Example meaning: “I thank my friend for helping.”
12. smile — 미소 짓다. Example meaning: “We smile at each other.”

Panel plan: Panel 1 must establish the setting and visibly demonstrate words 1–3. Panel 2 must introduce a new action or small problem and demonstrate words 4–6. Panel 3 must show a different camera angle and demonstrate words 7–9. Panel 4 must resolve the event and demonstrate words 10–12. If a word is abstract, show it through a concrete gesture, object, spatial relationship, or consequence. Keep the scene specific to this Day and unlike every other Day.
